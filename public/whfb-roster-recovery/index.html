Recover your saved rosters

The builder's new home is whfb.drowsy.games. Browser saves stay with their original website. This page reads the saves on jeremyglebe.com without changing or deleting them.

  1. Open this page in the same browser, profile, and device you used for the old builder.
  2. Download each roster below.
  3. Open the new builder, choose Import, and select each downloaded .whfb8roster file. Check your rosters before clearing any old browser data.

If your rosters were already synced to your account, sign in on the new site first. Import only missing rosters: importing creates a new copy and may duplicate a cloud roster.
